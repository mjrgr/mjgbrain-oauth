Privacy policy

mjgbrain is a personal tool used only by its owner, with his own Google account.

  • Google user data is accessed only on the owner's own machine, at his request.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on that machine and are never shared.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions.

Use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
